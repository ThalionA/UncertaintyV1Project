## 0. Corrections made to the auditors (verified here)

Three auditor claims were wrong or overstated; recording them so they don't propagate.

1. **`shape_lambda`'s missing renormalisation does NOT invalidate the cure.** The bug is real
   — `run_experiment.py:214` adds λ without renormalising, so `sum(evar)` = 1+91λ (verified
   from saved weights: 1.91 / 3.73 / 10.1 / **28.3** for shape 1/3/10/30; `flat_evar` L212 and
   `evar_alpha` L221 both renormalise). But `(evar+λ)/(1+nλ) ∝ (evar+λ)`, so the **relative**
   per-PC weighting — the whole scientific content — is identical either way; `min(evar)` = λ
   exactly, as intended. Adam is ~invariant to a constant loss rescale, so the only real
   consequence is effective weight-decay dilution (`optim.Adam` adds `wd·θ` pre-scaling). And
   the wd axis rules that out as the explanation: **wd=0 leaves peakiness at 0.232/0.692**, far
   from the cured 0.057/0.058. → Fix the renormalisation for provenance/comparability and
   re-run one scale-matched cell to be airtight, but the conclusion stands.
2. **`pca_basis` is `all_trials`, not `condition_mean`.** All **401** on-disk `config.yaml`
   files (incl. `loss_comparison_v1`, `hpsweep_wide`, `hpsweep_v2`) use `all_trials`, and the
   `Config` default is `all_trials`. **`GOTCHAS.md:29` and `:64` are stale** — they assert the
   condition-averaged basis and hence the "stim_mean beats the decoder is a near-tautology"
   framing, which misled both the vault audit (its "sharpest circularity threat") and the stats
   audit (A17 "the tautology is the default path"). A *weaker* concern survives — with
   `all_trials` the leading PCs are still dominated by across-condition structure — so the
   residual-basis control remains the right test, but the tautology as written applies to no run
   on disk. **Fix GOTCHAS.**
3. **Reconciling the two auditors on basis leakage.** No *test* leakage (stats audit correct:
   z-scoring and the basis are train-only, computed post-carve, and the reported metric comes
   only from `test_indices`). But the code audit is also correct that for `loss_comparison_v1`
   (`val_frac: 0.0, patience: 15`) the early-stopping holdout is carved *inside* `fit_model`
   from trials whose targets already entered the basis — so the **stopping-epoch choice** is
   measured in a basis fit partly on its own holdout. Mild, and confined to epoch selection.

## 2. CRITICAL — could change a reported result

**C1. Restart selection on *training* loss is a live confound for the project's central product.**
`nn_classifier.py:828-835` picks the best of 5 restarts by training loss. Known and deliberately
unfixed — but the headline claims are *about* overfitting and over-sharpening, and this rule
biases toward the sharper/more-overfit restart **more strongly for richer objectives**, which is
exactly the KL-overfits-most / PCA-overfits-least ordering that was explained mechanistically.
*Remedy: one `REP=1` control cell per loss; if the val/train ordering holds, the confound is retired.*

**C2. The predict-mean null is fit in-sample on the targets it scores** (`predict_mean_baseline.py:116`),
and it is **anti-conservative in exactly the direction of the headlines**: it understates the null
loss, which makes "worse than chance" claims *easier*. Claims within ~5% of 1.0 currently have no
defensible sign — including "KL/JS beat chance" and DeepSets' 0.990/0.993/1.004. The `shape_lambda`
cure (0.78/0.75) is ~20% clear, so it survives.
*Remedy exists in-repo and is unused: `decoder_metrics.variance_baseline` already takes a `train_target_mean`.*

**C3. The shuffle null is a single fixed permutation per mouse**, deterministic only by accident —
`utils.py:35` mutates the **global** numpy RNG and `run_experiment.py:490` inherits it. Every
shuffle-normalised denominator carries un-averaged permutation noise, perfectly correlated across
all cells within a mouse. *Remedy: dedicated `default_rng`, average over ≥20 permutations.*

**C4. Two CE implementations disagree by up to 1.3 nats.** Training-side `eps=1.19e-7`
(`nn_classifier.py:20`) makes CE **saturate at 15.94 nats** — loss *and gradient* capped for
confidently-wrong predictions; `pca_loss.py:177` uses `1e-12` under a comment claiming they match.
Tests pin numpy↔torch agreement for the PCA branch only. *Remedy: `F.log_softmax` on logits + an
agreement test for CE/KL/JS/Wasserstein.*

**C5. A surviving duplicate PCA loss in `time_binned_ppc.py:258` is exactly 100× the canonical one**
and silently falls back to MSE when `pcs is None` — the two failure modes `pca_loss.py` exists to
prevent. *Remedy: delete, import `pca_loss.pca_distance`.*

**C6. torch is never seeded in the production path** (no `manual_seed` in `training/run.py`,
`run_experiment.py`, `nn_classifier.py`). Init and restart sequence differ every rerun; with
`skip_existing=True`, mice within one `.mat` aren't exchangeable.

**C7. `run_recovery_Q_spyder.py` does destructive work at import** — no `__main__` guard,
`FORCE_RERUN=True`, `shutil.move`s a 116 MB cache and launches training. Any import (test
collector, IDE index) triggers it. Open since 2026-06-09.

## 6. Extension directions (ranked)

**E1. Ask whether V1 carries uncertainty *beyond stimulus condition*.** Three independent strands
converge here: the DeepSets result (**no model beats its within-condition null**; condition-mean
oracle ~20× better than any decoder), the residual-basis concern, and the vault's own unrecorded
implication. Much of the decoding programme may be measuring condition decoding wearing
uncertainty's clothes. *Diagnosis: zero new runs (on disk). Constructive version: retrain on
residual targets (`pca_basis='residual'`, already implemented) — a modest local re-run. Risk: low;
informative either way.* **Settle this before further loss engineering.**

**E2. Make PPC-vs-sampling diagnostic, or retire the framing.** `GOTCHAS` already states the two
architectures differ only in whether time-averaging precedes or follows the softmax — canonical PPC
needs no MLP, canonical SBC no per-bin softmax — *and* the spat/temp ratio's chance floor is above
1.0 for pure Monte-Carlo reasons (1.8–1.9× with an untrained random MLP). The discriminating test is
not "which readout decodes better" but whether within-trial variability carries uncertainty beyond
the trial mean — which DeepSets operationalised properly and which came out null. *Demote
"PPC"/"SBC" to "spatial/temporal readout" in all prose regardless.*

**E3. Break the n=6 ceiling with a hierarchical model over the 29 sessions** (6 mice, 4–5 sessions
each, already recorded). Mouse as random intercept legitimately uses 29 sessions without claiming 29
animals. Caveat, and it is the project's own: this will **not** manufacture significance for effects
that disagree in sign across mice — it will correctly report heterogeneity, plus a variance
decomposition that turns "n=6 is underpowered" into a quantified statement. *Re-analysis only.*

**E4. Complement the decoder with an encoding model.** A decoder's conclusion is bounded by its
inductive bias, and this project demonstrated that vividly: the *same data* gives "over-sharpened
4.3×" or "calibrated" depending only on the loss. Fit `p(r|s)`, invert, compare the implied posterior
to the ideal observer — no trained readout in the loop. *New modelling, existing data; moderate risk
(~350 trials/mouse limits a rich `p(r|s)`; start with tuning curves + noise covariance + linear
Fisher information).* Ishan is already assigned the encoding direction.

**E5. Fix the nulls and re-report the boundary cells before anything ships** (C1+C2+held-out
validation of the chosen `shape_lambda`). Every claim within ~5% of 1.0 currently has no defensible
sign. *One afternoon of re-analysis + one cheap control run.*

**E6. The cleanest publishable claim today — a methods paper on loss geometry.** *A projection/
location-based training loss systematically over-sharpens decoded posteriors, and this is loss
mis-specification, not overfitting — the two are dissociable, and only a loss-side term fixes it.*
It has the most complete evidence chain the project owns: a closed-form mechanism, a toy proof, a
round-trip control that removes the gap while the 4.33× over-sharpening persists, four correct
sequential predictions (λ_H sharpens, dropout no help, weight averaging no help, smoothness works),
the predicted cure delivered, **and** a cautionary counter-example (`weight_decay` looks cured on the
proxy and is a dead uniform decoder on the real metric). It needs neither the PPC/SBC framing nor
n>6, and generalises well beyond mouse V1. *Exposure: E5 first, and always report normalised loss
beside peakiness.*

**E7. No-hidden-layer decoder** (meeting item #6, unrun) — the mechanism note attributes the
sharpening drift to the softmax Jacobian and shared weights, *not* capacity, so a linear decoder
should still drift peaky. Cheapest remaining real-data test separating bias from variance.

**E8. Two-interrogation-time data** for the DDM prediction — σ_v is a flat likelihood ridge at a
single T, so this is a next-cohort data ask, not analysis. Listed to stop it consuming analysis time.
